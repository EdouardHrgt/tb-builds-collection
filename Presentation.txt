# ⚔️ Sigmar's Arsenal
**A build-sharing site made for the Vermintide 2 Modded Realm community.**

**What you can do**
- 🔎 Browse builds, filter by **hero, career or author**, sort by newest, oldest or most liked
- 🏆 Three lists: **Top rated**, **From people I follow**, **All builds**
- 🎠 Carousels you can drag, with a "see all" window. Hide any list you don't need
- 📜 Full build page: **talents, weapons with traits and properties, equipment, breakpoints, ideal team**
- 🔗 Share a build by link, or export it as formatted text
- 🛠️ Create, edit and delete your own builds, checked against the game data
- ❤️ Like, 🔖 bookmark (private), 💬 comment and ➕ follow other players
- 👤 Public profiles, with a follower count you can hide
- 🎭 24 avatars to choose from
- 🏠 A personal page with your builds, bookmarks and the players you follow
